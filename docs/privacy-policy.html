Dustline - Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 November 2026

Summary: Dustline is a single-player game. Your game data (name, progress, settings) stays on your device. The game shows optional rewarded video ads provided by Google AdMob; to do this, Google may collect certain device information (see section 4). There are no in-app purchases, no accounts on our servers and no analytics of our own.

1. Who we are

Dustline (package com.dustline.game) is published by YOUR NAME OR STUDIO NAME. Contact: YOUR_EMAIL.

2. What is stored on your device

  • the display name you type and a player ID that the game generates;
  • your chosen character, outfit and profile settings;
  • game progress, coins, diamonds and match statistics;
  • settings such as graphics options, sensitivity and HUD layout;
  • pictures you take with the in-game photo album, kept in the app’s private storage.

We do not operate a server for the game, and the app does not send this information to us.

3. Permissions

  • INTERNET and ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: only to load and show the optional rewarded video ads;
  • com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID: lets Google AdMob use your device’s advertising ID for advertising and fraud prevention. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android Settings > Privacy > Ads;
  • VIBRATE: vibration during play.

The app does not request storage, camera, microphone, contacts or location permissions.

4. Advertising (Google AdMob)

Dustline contains optional rewarded video ads (the “WATCH VIDEO” button) served by Google AdMob. When an ad is requested, Google may collect and process: your device’s advertising ID, IP address, device and operating-system information, app information and your interactions with ads. Google uses this to deliver and measure ads, to personalise ads where permitted and to prevent fraud and abuse. We do not receive this data and do not sell it.

Ads are only requested if you are online and, where the law requires it (for example in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland), after you have given consent in the consent form shown by Google’s User Messaging Platform. You can change your choice at any time in the game under Settings > ACCOUNT > AD PRIVACY CHOICES. You can also limit ad personalisation in your Android settings.

How Google uses data from apps that use its services: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites. Google Privacy Policy: policies.google.com/privacy.

The game has no other third-party SDKs, no analytics service and no crash-reporting service.

5. Sharing that you choose

The profile screen has Copy and Share buttons. Copy places a short text with your profile details on the clipboard. Share opens the standard Android share sheet with the same text; nothing is sent unless you pick an app and a recipient yourself.

6. Device backups

If backups are turned on in your Android settings, Android may include the game’s saved progress in your device or Google-account backup so it can be restored on a new phone. This is controlled by your device and your Google account, not by us.

7. Deleting your data

In the game: Settings > ACCOUNT > DELETE ALL MY DATA erases everything the game stored on your device. Uninstalling the game, or choosing Clear storage for the app in Android settings, does the same. Backups made by Android can be managed in your Google account settings. Data that Google AdMob processes is governed by Google’s policies linked above.

8. Children

Dustline is intended for players aged 13 and over and is not directed to children under 13. The game contains simulated gun combat against computer-controlled characters. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has used the game, contact us at the address above.

9. Your rights

Because your game data stays on your device, you can view, change and delete it yourself in the game. Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or CCPA/CPRA) you may have additional rights regarding data processed by Google; use the controls described in section 4 or contact Google. For questions to us, write to the contact address below.
