## Sketchfab models: how to make them safe WITHOUT removing them
1. Put the original downloads (.glb) into `reference/sketchfab/`. For a model you only have baked, add a line to `tools/extra_credits.txt`.
2. Run `python3 tools/extract_credits.py`. It reads author / licence / link from each file, writes `CREDITS.md` and `assets/credits.txt` (shown in the game under Settings > ACCOUNT > CREDITS & LICENSES) and prints a verdict per model.
3. Verdicts:
   - **OK** (CC0, CC-BY): allowed in an app with ads, you only have to give credit (done by step 2).
   - **CHECK** (CC-BY-SA or no licence): read the licence page; share-alike can force the model files to stay open.
   - **STOP** (NonCommercial, NoDerivatives, Editorial, Standard): not allowed here (the app has ads and the models are modified / baked). Replace the model, or get written permission from the author.
4. Also paste the same credit lines into the Play Store full description.
5. Do not use models that were ripped from other games (not from Sketchfab creators), even if they sit on Sketchfab.
